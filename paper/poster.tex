\column{0.42}
\block{The problem}{
  How a language model handles a fact is studied by \textbf{five separate
  communities} that rarely cite one another.
  \vspace{6mm}

  \resizebox{\linewidth}{!}{%
  \begin{tikzpicture}
    \foreach \n [count=\i from 0] in
      {retrieval-augmented generation, knowledge editing, continual learning,
       context engineering, agent memory}
      \node[anchor=west, text=ink] at (1.3, -\i*1.45) {\n};
    \foreach \c [count=\i from 0] in {acquire, store, retrieve, update, forget}
      \fill[\c] (0, -\i*1.45 - 0.22) rectangle (0.72, -\i*1.45 + 0.34);
    \useasboundingbox (0,-6.3) rectangle (21, 0.6);
  \end{tikzpicture}}
  \vspace{7mm}

  Each has its own benchmarks, and each passes them. A system can pass
  \textbf{all five} and still fail, because nothing tests what happens
  \textbf{between} them.
  \vspace{7mm}

  \resizebox{\linewidth}{!}{%
  \begin{tikzpicture}
    \fill[panel]   (0,0) rectangle (30, 5.6);
    \fill[acquire] (0,0) rectangle (0.40, 5.6);
    \node[anchor=west, align=left, text width=27cm] at (1.5, 2.8)
      {Across \textbf{20 representative papers} the median covers \textbf{two}
       of the five stages.};
  \end{tikzpicture}}
}

\column{0.58}
\block{The framework}{
  A fact passes through five stages. Naming them as one lifecycle is what makes
  the boundaries between them visible.
  \vspace{7mm}

  \resizebox{\linewidth}{!}{%
  \begin{tikzpicture}[
    stage/.style={rounded corners=3pt, minimum width=7.8cm, minimum height=4.2cm,
                  text=white, font=\bfseries\Large, align=center},
    flow/.style={-{Stealth[length=6mm, width=4mm]}, line width=3pt, muted}
  ]
    \node[stage, fill=acquire]  (a) at (0,0)    {Acquire};
    \node[stage, fill=store]    (s) at (9.8,0)  {Store};
    \node[stage, fill=retrieve] (r) at (19.6,0) {Retrieve};
    \node[stage, fill=update]   (u) at (29.4,0) {Update};
    \node[stage, fill=forget]   (f) at (39.2,0) {Forget};

    \draw[flow] (a) -- (s);
    \draw[flow] (s) -- (r);
    \draw[flow] (r) -- (u);
    \draw[flow] (u) -- (f);

    \node[below=4mm of a, text=muted] {training};
    \node[below=4mm of s, text=muted] {weights, index};
    \node[below=4mm of r, text=muted] {attention, RAG};
    \node[below=4mm of u, text=muted] {editing, tuning};
    \node[below=4mm of f, text=muted] {unlearning};

    % The conflict spans two stages, so it is a brace beneath them rather than a
    % label on the arrow between them, where it collided with the Update box.
    \draw[update, line width=3pt, decorate,
          decoration={brace, mirror, amplitude=8mm}]
      ([xshift=-3.9cm, yshift=-2.7cm]r.south) -- ([xshift=3.9cm, yshift=-2.7cm]u.south);
    \node[text=update, font=\bfseries\Large] at (24.5, -6.6) {conflict};
    \useasboundingbox (-4.5,-7.8) rectangle (43.7, 2.6);
  \end{tikzpicture}}
  \vspace{7mm}

  The failure below sits between \textcolor{retrieve}{\textbf{Retrieve}} and
  \textcolor{update}{\textbf{Update}}: the document arrives, and nothing tells
  the model which copy of the fact to trust. That boundary belongs to neither
  field beside it, which is why it goes untested.
}

\block{The finding}{
  Vioxx was withdrawn worldwide in September 2004 for raising the risk of heart
  attack and stroke. Put that withdrawal notice \textbf{directly into GPT-2's
  prompt}, then ask whether the drug is safe to prescribe.

\begin{columns}
\column{0.34}
\block{A metric}{
  \textbf{Lifecycle Desynchronization} is the surprisal of the \textbf{correct}
  answer while the corrective document is present:
  \vspace{4mm}
  \begin{equation*}
    \widehat{\mathcal{D}}_{sync} = -\ln P\!\left(y^{*} \mid q,\, \theta_{stale},\, \mathcal{M}_{now}\right)
  \end{equation*}
  \vspace{2mm}

  In nats: $n$ nats means probability $e^{-n}$. Past \textbf{9.2} it is below one
  chance in ten thousand.
  \vspace{7mm}

  \resizebox{\linewidth}{!}{%
  \begin{tikzpicture}
    \fill[ok!30]     (0,0)    rectangle (2.3, 1.5);
    \fill[yellow!45] (2.3,0)  rectangle (9.2, 1.5);
    \fill[orange!45] (9.2,0)  rectangle (16.1, 1.5);
    \fill[update!45] (16.1,0) rectangle (24, 1.5);
    \draw[ink, line width=5pt] (21.1, -0.5) -- (21.1, 2.0);
    \node[text=muted, font=\small] at (2.3, -1.3)  {0.7};
    \node[text=muted, font=\small] at (9.2, -1.3)  {4.6};
    \node[text=muted, font=\small] at (16.1, -1.3) {9.2};
    \node[font=\bfseries] at (21.1, -1.3) {12.05};
    \useasboundingbox (0,-2.1) rectangle (24, 2.4);
  \end{tikzpicture}}
  \vspace{7mm}

  A companion number, \textbf{context influence}, asks whether the document moved
  the model at all. Here it is \textbf{0.033} nats: present, and \textbf{inert}.
}

\column{0.34}
\block{An architecture}{
  Storage records \textbf{what} is true but never \textbf{when} it was learned,
  so nothing lets the model prefer fresh evidence over a confident old memory.
  \vspace{7mm}

  \resizebox{\linewidth}{!}{%
  \begin{tikzpicture}[
    slot/.style={rounded corners=3pt, minimum width=7.2cm, minimum height=3.4cm,
                 align=center, draw=hairline, line width=2.5pt, font=\Large}
  ]
    \node[slot] (k) at (0,0)   {key $k_i$};
    \node[slot] (v) at (8.6,0) {value $v_i$};
    \node[slot, draw=acquire, fill=acquire!12, text=acquire] (p) at (17.2,0)
      {\textbf{provenance} $p_i$};
    \draw[-{Stealth[length=5mm]}, muted, line width=2.5pt] (k) -- (v);
    \draw[-{Stealth[length=5mm]}, acquire, line width=3.5pt] (v) -- (p);
    \node[below=3mm of p, text=acquire, font=\small] {what we add};
    \useasboundingbox (-4,-3.6) rectangle (21.2, 2);
  \end{tikzpicture}}
  \vspace{7mm}

  The \textbf{Provenance Vector} attaches acquisition time to every memory slot.
  A gate reads it at inference and mutes facts that have gone stale:
  \vspace{3mm}
  \begin{equation*}
    S(\Delta\tau_i) = \sigma(\gamma - \beta\,\Delta\tau_i)
  \end{equation*}
  \vspace{3mm}

  \textbf{Weights are never modified}, so the specificity bottleneck that limits
  static editing does not apply. Under \textbf{0.02\%} extra parameters.
}
