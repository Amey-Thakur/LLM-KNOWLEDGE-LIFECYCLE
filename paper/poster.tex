\block{The problem}{
  Research on how a language model handles a fact is split across
  \textbf{five separate communities} that rarely cite one another.
  \vspace{8mm}

  \resizebox{\linewidth}{!}{%
  \begin{tikzpicture}[font=\sffamily]
    \foreach \n [count=\i from 0] in
      {retrieval-augmented generation, knowledge editing, continual learning,
       context engineering, agent memory}
      \node[anchor=west, text=ink] at (1.4, -\i*1.5) {\n};
    \foreach \c [count=\i from 0] in {acquire, store, retrieve, update, forget}
      \fill[\c] (0, -\i*1.5 - 0.25) rectangle (0.75, -\i*1.5 + 0.35);
    \useasboundingbox (0,-6.6) rectangle (22, 0.6);
  \end{tikzpicture}}
  \vspace{10mm}

  Each has its own benchmarks, and each passes them. A deployed system can pass
  \textbf{all five} and still fail, because nothing tests what happens
  \textbf{between} them.
  \vspace{10mm}

  \resizebox{\linewidth}{!}{%
  \begin{tikzpicture}
    \fill[panel] (0,0) rectangle (30, 7.2);
    \fill[acquire] (0,0) rectangle (0.42, 7.2);
    \node[anchor=west, align=left, text width=27cm, font=\sffamily] at (1.6, 3.6)
      {Across \textbf{20 representative papers}, the median covers \textbf{two}
       of the five stages. The boundaries are where deployment breaks, and where
       almost nobody is looking.};
  \end{tikzpicture}}
}

\column{0.56}
\block{The framework}{
  A fact passes through five stages. Naming them as one lifecycle is what makes
  the boundaries between them visible.
  \vspace{10mm}

  \resizebox{\linewidth}{!}{%
  \begin{tikzpicture}[

  The failure measured on this poster sits between
  \textcolor{retrieve}{\textbf{Retrieve}} and \textcolor{update}{\textbf{Update}}:
  the document arrives, and nothing in the architecture tells the model which
  copy of the fact to trust. That boundary belongs to neither field beside it,
  which is exactly why it goes untested.
}

\begin{columns}
\column{0.5}
\block{Contribution 1: a metric}{
  \textbf{Lifecycle Desynchronization} is the surprisal of the \textbf{correct}
  answer while the corrective document is present:
  \vspace{6mm}
  \begin{equation*}
    \widehat{\mathcal{D}}_{sync}
      = -\ln P\!\left(y^{*} \mid q,\, \theta_{stale},\, \mathcal{M}_{now}\right)
  \end{equation*}
  \vspace{4mm}

  Measured in nats: $n$ nats means the correct answer holds probability
  $e^{-n}$. Past \textbf{9.2} nats it is below one chance in ten thousand, where
  no realistic decoding recovers it.
  \vspace{10mm}

  \resizebox{\linewidth}{!}{%
  \begin{tikzpicture}[font=\sffamily]
    \fill[ok!30]     (0,0)  rectangle (2.3, 1.5);
    \fill[yellow!45] (2.3,0) rectangle (9.2, 1.5);
    \fill[orange!45] (9.2,0) rectangle (16.1, 1.5);
    \fill[update!45] (16.1,0) rectangle (24, 1.5);
    \draw[ink, line width=5pt] (21.1, -0.5) -- (21.1, 2.0);
    \node[text=muted, font=\sffamily\small] at (2.3, -1.4)  {0.7};
    \node[text=muted, font=\sffamily\small] at (9.2, -1.4)  {4.6};
    \node[text=muted, font=\sffamily\small] at (16.1, -1.4) {9.2};
    \node[font=\sffamily\bfseries] at (21.1, -1.4) {12.05};
    \node[anchor=west, text=muted, font=\sffamily\small] at (0, 2.9) {synchronized};
    \node[anchor=east, text=muted, font=\sffamily\small] at (24, 2.9) {unrecoverable};
    \useasboundingbox (0,-2.2) rectangle (24, 3.6);
  \end{tikzpicture}}
  \vspace{12mm}

  A companion diagnostic, \textbf{context influence}, asks whether the document
  moved the model \textit{at all}. On this probe it is \textbf{0.033} nats: the
  document is present and \textbf{inert}. The pair separates a retrieval failure
  from a resolution failure, which no single-stage benchmark can do.
}

\column{0.5}
\block{Contribution 2: an architecture}{
  Parametric storage records \textbf{what} is true but never \textbf{when} it was
  learned or \textbf{where} it came from, so at inference there is no principled
  basis for preferring fresh evidence over a confident old memory.
  \vspace{10mm}

  \resizebox{\linewidth}{!}{%
  \begin{tikzpicture}[
    font=\sffamily,
    slot/.style={rounded corners=3pt, minimum width=7.4cm, minimum height=3.6cm,
                 align=center, draw=hairline, line width=2.5pt, font=\sffamily\Large}
  ]
    \node[slot] (k) at (0,0)    {key $k_i$};
    \node[slot] (v) at (9,0)    {value $v_i$};
    \node[slot, draw=acquire, fill=acquire!12, text=acquire] (p) at (18,0)
      {\textbf{provenance} $p_i$};
    \draw[-{Stealth[length=5mm]}, muted, line width=2.5pt] (k) -- (v);
    \draw[-{Stealth[length=5mm]}, acquire, line width=3.5pt] (v) -- (p);
    \node[below=4mm of k, text=muted, font=\sffamily\small] {stored today};
    \node[below=4mm of p, text=acquire, font=\sffamily\small] {what we add};
    \useasboundingbox (-4.2,-4.4) rectangle (22.2, 2.2);
  \end{tikzpicture}}
  \vspace{12mm}

  The \textbf{Provenance Vector} attaches acquisition time and source reliability
  to every memory slot. A gate reads that metadata at inference and attenuates
  facts that have gone stale:
  \vspace{4mm}
  \begin{equation*}
    \text{FFN}_{steered}(x) = \sum_{i=1}^{d_m} S(\Delta\tau_i)\, f(x \cdot k_i)\, v_i,
    \qquad S(\Delta\tau_i) = \sigma(\gamma - \beta\,\Delta\tau_i)
  \end{equation*}
  \vspace{4mm}

  Fresh facts pass with $S \approx 1$; stale facts are muted toward $0$.
  \textbf{The weights are never modified}, so the specificity bottleneck that
  limits static editing does not apply. Cost: under \textbf{0.02\%} extra
  parameters on a 7B model.
}
\end{columns}
